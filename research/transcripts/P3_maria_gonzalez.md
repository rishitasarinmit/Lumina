**Interviewer (Q2):** Tell me about a time during recovery when something felt "off." What did you notice, what did you do, and how did it turn out?

**Maria:** With my mom, yesterday, a little. She was kind of foggy when I came in the morning, repeating herself, and her face was flushed. **Interviewer:** You said "I think." Tell me more about that.

**Maria:** *(pause)* Because of my dad. Six years ago my dad had his gallbladder out, and nine days later he died of septic shock. **Interviewer:** How did you feel after the camera alerts, once you turned them off?

**Maria:** Relieved, and guilty. Relieved because I could sleep, guilty because what if one of those times it's her on the floor. That's what I'm like. The first false alarm, I panic. The third one, I stop believing it. I know that about myself. My husband says I'm either at a ten or a zero.
